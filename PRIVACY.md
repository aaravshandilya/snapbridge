# SNAPBridge Privacy Statement

Last updated: October 25, 2026

SNAPBridge is an independent SNAP application-preparation demonstration. It is not a government service and does not submit benefit applications.

## Information used

The app uses household size and composition, state, approximate income, income frequency, employment category, and selected household expenses to personalize the preparation workflow. It does not need and should never request Social Security numbers, bank credentials, account passwords, or identity-document images.

## Storage and sharing

Responses are held in temporary, per-user Streamlit session memory. The app does not intentionally log responses, write them to shared files or a database, or send them to an external AI service. The public MVP has no applicant accounts and no document-upload feature.

When the session expires or the user selects **Clear My Information**, the app no longer retains those responses in session memory. A user may download a PDF worksheet or CSV checklist; those files are then controlled by the user and their device.

## Official applications

When a user follows a link to Health-e-Arizona Plus or another official resource, that website's privacy and security practices apply. SNAPBridge does not receive confirmation of submission, approval, or case status.

## Future features

Accounts, cloud storage, uploads, and government integrations require a separate security phase with authentication, encryption, access controls, audit practices, retention and deletion policies, incident response planning, and an independent privacy/security review.
